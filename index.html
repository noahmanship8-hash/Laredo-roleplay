Rules

The rules

Read these before you play. They apply in the game and in our Discord.

General regulations

  1. Respect all players and staff at all times.
  2. No harassment, discrimination or hate speech of any kind.
  3. Follow all staff instructions immediately.
  4. No advertising other servers or communities.
  5. No exploiting, glitching or using third-party cheats.

Roleplay regulations

  1. Stay in character at all times while in the game.
  2. No Fail RP: act realistically for every situation.
  3. No Random Deathmatch (RDM) or Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM).
  4. No Combat Logging: do not leave to avoid a scene.
  5. Value your life: do not act recklessly when threatened.

Vehicle regulations

  1. Drive realistically and obey traffic laws.
  2. No reckless driving or purposely crashing into others.
  3. Emergency vehicles may only use lights and sirens on a call.

Discord regulations

  1. Keep chats on topic and use the correct channels.
  2. No spamming, mass pinging or excessive caps.
  3. No NSFW or graphic content.
  4. Do not ping staff inside tickets, they are already notified.

Privacy

What we keep

This site only asks for what it needs to run applications and the staff panel.

When you sign in with Discord

We receive your Discord ID, display name and avatar. We check which roles you have in the Laredo Roleplay server each time you open something that needs a role. We do not read your messages or your other servers, and we never see your password.

When you link Roblox

Roblox gives us your Roblox ID and username. That is all. You can unlink at any time from your dashboard.

Applications

Your answers, your Discord name and ID, and your linked Roblox account (if any) are stored so staff can review them. Staff with panel access can read them.

Cookies and storage

One cookie keeps you signed in for seven days. Your Normal or Halloween choice is saved in your browser. There is no advertising or tracking.

Removing your data

Ask in a ticket in our Discord and a member of leadership will remove it.
